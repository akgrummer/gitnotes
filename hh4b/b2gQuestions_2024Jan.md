- In order to understand the compatibility of the observed excess with the targeted signal, we would like to see the following quick studies:
    1. Please produce a plot comparing the post-fit nuisance parameter pulls between the S+B and the B-only fits. This can easily done with combine. Please order the nuisance parameters by how differently the parameters are pulled in both fits to easily identify the parameters most affected.
    2. Please include the most significant signal mass point in the distributions. The distributions should then show the result of the S+B fit for both B and S.
    2a. Please also show a comparison of the data/fit (either B or S+B, depending on the fit) for both the S+B and B-only fit. The aim is to understand where the S+B fit describes the data significantly better than the B-only fit.
    3. Please overlay in the limit plots how the most significant signal would look like. To do this, you can create pseudo-data by injecting into your background the corresponding signal and fitting to those pseudo-data instead of real data. This will again help us understand how compatible the excess observed is with the expected signal.
